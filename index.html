Distance Sampling

Survey design

Line-transect distance sampling: an observer traverses a transect and detects animals with probability declining with perpendicular distance. Detected distances are used to fit a detection function and estimate density.

  • Live observer traversal and one-shot detections
  • Half-normal and hazard-rate detection functions
  • Running density estimates and model-mismatch experiments

SECR

Capture-recapture

Spatially explicit capture-recapture: a detector array samples animals whose latent activity centres drive detection probability across repeated occasions. Density is estimated from the resulting spatial capture histories.

  • Detector arrays and activity-centre-based detection
  • Repeated sampling occasions with movement fidelity controls
  • Surface, convergence, and capture-history analytics

Lotka-Volterra

Dynamics

Three two-species dynamical systems integrated via RK4: classic predator-prey, dynamic predator-prey with logistic prey growth and predator self-limitation, and two-species competition.

  • Classic, dynamic, and competition model variants
  • Live time-series and phase-plane views
  • Preset-driven exploration of contrasting behaviours
