How the modules differ

Distance Sampling

Best for teaching detectability from line-transect distances and the move from sightings to density estimation.

SECR

Best for teaching spatial capture histories, repeated occasions, and the role of latent activity centres.

Lotka-Volterra

Best for teaching mechanism-driven population dynamics, equilibria, cycles, and competitive exclusion.
