# Glossary

## Core symbols

- D: true density (animals per square kilometre)
- D_hat: estimated density
- sigma: detection-scale parameter controlling distance falloff
- sigma_hat: estimated sigma from data
- W: truncation distance in DS
- ESW: effective strip width in DS
- g0: baseline detection probability at zero distance in SECR
- K: number of occasions in SECR
- ESA: effective sampling area

## DS terms

- Transect: survey line traversed by the observer.
- Perpendicular distance: shortest distance from animal to transect.
- g(0) = 1 assumption: certainty of detection exactly on the transect line.

## SECR terms

- Activity centre: latent centre of an individual's home range.
- Detector layout: placement pattern for detectors (grid/random/custom).
- Capture history: record of who was detected, where, and when.

## LV terms

- Prey: resource species in predator-prey modes.
- Predator: consumer species in predator-prey modes.
- Competition coefficients (alpha12, alpha21): strength of inter-species crowding in competition mode.
- Carrying capacity: population ceiling from density dependence.
